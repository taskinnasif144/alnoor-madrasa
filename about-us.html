    The Al-Noor Madrasa & Islamic Complex

    The Al-Noor Madrasa and Islamic Complex is a prominent Islamic educational institution located in Sarishabari, Mymensingh District, Bangladesh. Established in 2005, our institution has been serving the community with dedication for nearly two decades.

    Our madrasa was founded with the vision of providing quality Islamic education alongside modern skills, nurturing students who are grounded in faith while being equipped to contribute positively to society. The institution stands as a beacon of knowledge and spiritual development in the region.

    The complex includes dedicated classrooms for Hifz (Quran memorization), Nazira (Quran reading), Arabic language studies, and Islamic jurisprudence. Our faculty consists of qualified scholars and teachers who are committed to excellence in education.

    Our Mission & Values

    • Providing accessible Islamic education to students of all backgrounds
    • Nurturing spiritual growth alongside academic excellence
    • Serving the community through charity, welfare, and outreach programs
    • Maintaining transparency in governance and fund management
    • Preserving and promoting authentic Islamic knowledge and traditions

    Key Facts

    Our institution operates under a dedicated management committee with representation from local scholars and community leaders. We currently serve over 200 students with 25 qualified teachers, providing not only education but also meals and accommodation for residential students.

    Through the generous support of donors and community members, we continue to expand our programs and reach more students in need. Our vision is to become a leading center of Islamic learning that combines traditional scholarship with modern educational approaches.
