    Nurul Quran Hafizia Madrasa

    The Nurul Quran Hafizia Madrasa is the flagship educational program of Al-Noor Islamic Complex. Established on September 4, 2000, this specialized institution is dedicated to the memorization of the Holy Quran (Hifz) for orphan and underprivileged children.

    Our Hafizia program provides comprehensive care for its students including food, clothing, lodging, and medical facilities — all completely free of charge. The program prioritizes orphan children for admission, ensuring that those most in need receive both spiritual education and material support.

    Currently, 130 students are enrolled in the Hafizia program, learning under the guidance of 6 dedicated Hifz teachers, 1 Nazira teacher, 1 general teacher, and 2 support staff members. Each year, new students are admitted with preference given to orphans and children from disadvantaged backgrounds.

    Our Programs

    • Hifz Program: Complete memorization of the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed, typically completed in 2-3 years
    • Nazira Program: Learning to read the Quran correctly with proper pronunciation
    • Arabic Language: Foundation in Arabic grammar, vocabulary, and conversation
    • Islamic Studies: Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, and Aqeedah

    Annual Admission

    Every year, we conduct admission tests for new students. The admission process prioritizes orphan children and those from financially disadvantaged families. Interested families can contact the madrasa office during the admission period for details on requirements and schedules.

    We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to learn the Quran, regardless of their financial circumstances. Through the support of our donors and community members, we continue to provide this invaluable service to those who need it most.
