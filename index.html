    About Us

    Al-Noor Madrasa & Islamic Complex

    The Al-Noor Madrasa and Islamic Complex is a center of Islamic learning and community service located in Sarishabari, Mymensingh. Our institution combines traditional Islamic education with modern teaching methods to nurture well-rounded students.

    Founded on the principles of the Quran and Sunnah, we strive to serve the community through education, worship, and social welfare programs that benefit people of all ages.

    Established 2005
    200+ Students
    25 Teachers
